A movement open to everyone.

IA YAYA brings together artificial intelligence and the warmth of a "yaya", an affectionate Spanish word for grandmother. AI is that infinite 99.999; the 0.01 is infinite too. Both are symbols, not statistics. What is human will always be what identifies us.

The name is a loving play on words: "yaya" replaces "iaia" to create IA YAYA, which stands not for the 99.9% but for that uncountable 0.01%. It is what we call the "grandmother's knot": the affection, the crafts of care and the shared memories that no machine can replicate, like that childhood memory of how our grandmother lovingly tied our shoelaces. That "grandmother's knot" is never forgotten.

IA YAYA starts from a simple idea: what we know, remember and have lived also counts. Sharing it and talking about it can help us learn together. It is a virtual, global movement: you can join from anywhere, with the QR or the link.

1 · Share

Tell us something of yours.

Briefly tell us a piece of know-how, a memory or an experience of yours.

It does not have to be useful for anything in particular. No account needed. If you would like us to contact you or your contribution to be considered for weekly, monthly and annual recognition, leave an email address or phone number. You can still share without contact details, but your contribution will not be eligible for recognition.

For now, participation is from Spain, aged 14 or over. We are preparing a way for children under 14 to take part and for people in other countries to join, with no date yet. The website can be read from anywhere.
What happens to what you send

The non-profit initiative Global Startup Cities drives IA YAYA. The data controller is Global StartupCities, tax ID G74460304. You can write to us at arturo.villar@globalstartupcities.com.

We use your contribution to manage your participation in IA YAYA and the 0.01 Movement and to learn about the know-how and experiences you want to share. If you leave an email or a phone number, we will use it to reply to you about your contribution and, if selected, manage its weekly, monthly or annual recognition. Contact details are optional for sharing, but required to be eligible for recognition. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Without the contribution and your consent we cannot receive your participation through this form.

We will not publish your contribution or your contact details, or send you invitations to meetings, without asking your permission separately. Do not include other people’s personal details or information about health, beliefs, sex life or other private matters.

We use Tally as the form provider and data processor. Tally states that it stores responses in Europe and uses providers to deliver the service. Its agreement covers international transfers where necessary, with the safeguards required by law. Processing terms: tally.so/help/data-processing-agreement. Tally privacy notice: tally.so/help/privacy-policy.

We will keep your contribution and your contact details, if you leave them, for a maximum of 12 months from submission, or until you ask for deletion where applicable. After deletion, technical copies may remain with the provider for up to 90 days, according to Tally’s information.

You can ask for access, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of processing, portability and, where applicable, objection. You can also withdraw your consent, without affecting lawful use before withdrawal. Write to arturo.villar@globalstartupcities.com; if you left no contact, give us enough information to find your contribution, without sending unnecessary data. You can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

2 · Meet

And then, talk face to face.

This is the movement’s intention: to create moments where people meet, one to one or in groups. When one person tells and another listens, learning, ideas and even projects can arise. No screen or programme replaces that meeting. There are no dates or places yet.

3 · Recognition

We want to recognise contributions.

We want to recognise contributions every week, every month and every year, including virtually. We will soon share how.

4 · Origin

Where it comes from.

The 0.01 movement is born from IA YAYA, our proposal for the KNOWCASE call by the Cotec Foundation and Sónar+D (result still pending). Whatever the result, the movement is already under way. Thank you to the Cotec Foundation and Sónar+D for the call that set us in motion.

The 0.01 Movement is a project of Asturias50, launched from maréaTE. Thank you to Special Thanks Studios for the collaboration with which this idea began.
